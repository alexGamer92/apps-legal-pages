Chef Enzo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how Chef Enzo, a cooking-themed game published by Alexandru Struta (the “developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information. Contact: alexandrustruta@gmail.com.

Current scope: Chef Enzo is in development. As of the date above, the pre-release build saves progress locally, has no player accounts or developer-operated cloud saves, and does not serve advertisements or offer live purchases. Advertising through Unity LevelPlay and Apple subscriptions are planned; the sections below distinguish those planned features from current behavior. This policy must be updated before changed data practices are enabled.

1. Game progress on your device

The game stores progress, stars, coins, unlocked recipes, upgrades, outfits and cosmetics, reward dates and game-run markers on your device. These records make progress and rewards work and prevent duplicate rewards. The developer does not receive these save files automatically. There is no developer-operated account or cross-device progress synchronization. Device backups may contain app data according to your device and backup settings.

2. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request and investigate problems. Avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or unrelated sensitive information. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver and store email.

3. Planned purchases

When purchases become available, Apple will process payment through the App Store. The game uses Unity In-App Purchasing and Apple StoreKit to obtain product prices and transaction information, verify subscription status, restore purchases and respond to expiration or revocation. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple handles your Apple Account and billing information under its privacy policy.

Unity's purchasing service may process transaction and technical information under its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. Installing a purchasing package does not mean live purchasing is already available. The enabled service configuration and related disclosures will be reviewed before purchases launch.

4. Planned advertising and privacy choices

Unity LevelPlay has been selected for future rewarded and interstitial advertisements. It is not currently serving ads in Chef Enzo; no mediated advertising network is represented here as already enabled. Before advertisements are enabled, this policy will identify the actual providers, the information they process, purposes and available privacy controls.

Advertising services can process device identifiers, IP addresses, device information and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Personalized advertising may involve additional processing. See Unity's privacy policy for its services. This description of planned advertising is not a consent request. Any legally required consent and Apple's tracking permission will be handled before the relevant processing begins; refusing tracking permission must not prevent access to the free game.

5. Analytics and permissions

The current game does not implement player accounts, social sign-in, chat, a developer analytics service or cloud crash reporting. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. The operating system and app marketplace may separately process diagnostic and download information under their own settings and policies.

6. Reasons for processing and sharing

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we process support correspondence to respond to your request and for our legitimate interest in maintaining and supporting the game; we process information needed to provide a purchased feature to perform our agreement with you, and retain information where required by law. Consent is used where required for optional tracking or advertising processing. We do not sell support messages or local game saves. Information may be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights.

External providers may process information outside your country under their own applicable safeguards and policies. The linked Apple and Unity policies explain their handling of information and available rights.

7. Retention, deletion and your rights

Local progress remains on your device until removed or overwritten. Deleting the app and its data removes the local copy; offloading an app can preserve data, and device backups may retain copies. We cannot recover local progress from a server. Removing the app does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple's transaction records.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request, follow up on issues and meet applicable legal obligations; there is no fixed deletion period promised by this policy. You can email us to request access, correction or deletion of information we hold about you. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to restriction, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent and a complaint to your data protection authority. We may need to verify a request. Provider-held records and legally required retention may be subject to separate rules.

8. Audience and changes

Chef Enzo is intended for an adult audience and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and address it.

Updates will be posted here with a revised date. Material changes will receive additional notice or consent where required. For questions or privacy requests, email alexandrustruta@gmail.com.
