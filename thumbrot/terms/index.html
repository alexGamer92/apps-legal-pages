3. Progress, coins and streaks

Your progress is saved only on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud save, so deleting the app, losing your device or damaged data can lose your progress, and we cannot restore it. Restoring purchases brings back Premium and Remove Ads, not coins, streaks or other progress.

Coins and streak freezes are virtual items for use in the game. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money, sold or moved to another player. Coins never expire, including coins you buy. You earn coins by winning microgames, with a bonus on every 10th win in a row within a run, by completing the daily challenge and, if you choose to watch an ad, by doubling a run's coins. You can spend them to unlock microgame packs, to buy streak freezes or to keep a run going.

Your daily streak counts the calendar days (in your iPhone's time zone) on which you finish at least one microgame. If you miss days and hold enough streak freezes, one freeze is used for each missed day and the streak continues; otherwise the streak starts again and you keep your freezes.
