Help for Twenty Questions: Emoji on iPhone and iPad.

Contact us

To ask a question, report a problem or make a privacy request, open an issue on our public support tracker:

https://github.com/alexGamer92/apps-legal-pages/issues

You need a free GitHub account, and everything you write there is public. Please do not post passwords, purchase receipts, your sign-in details or other personal information. Describe the problem, your iPhone model and iOS version and the app version (shown in TestFlight or the App Store); for account or privacy requests, say so in the title and we will tell you how to continue without making your details public.

Common questions

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the Profile tab, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account, name, games, credits and leaderboard entries. If you signed in with Apple you are asked to confirm with Apple once more, so the app's access to your Apple ID is revoked.

I signed out and my credits or name are gone.

Signing out of an Apple or Google account keeps its data; sign in again with the same Apple or Google account to get it back. A guest account cannot be recovered after the guest signs out, and deleting an account removes everything for good.

My purchase did not add credits, or I want a refund.

Make sure you are signed in (guests cannot buy credits), then reopen the Shop tab. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple: request a refund from Apple or see your purchases at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple refunded a purchase, the credits are removed from your balance. If you were charged but got no credits, report it on the support tracker above.

How do hints, credits and practice games work?

In the app, open the Play tab and tap How to play. Every mode is free to play; credits pay for hints (the first one is free) and for extra practice games.

Which languages are supported?

English, Arabic, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Russian, Italian, Korean, Turkish, Indonesian and Hindi. Change the language in Profile → App language.
