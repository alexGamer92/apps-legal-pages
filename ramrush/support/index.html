RAM Rush Support

Help for RAM Rush on iPhone.

Contact us

For help, bug reports, privacy requests or questions, email Alexandru Struta at alexandrustruta@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, the iOS version (Settings › General › About) and the RAM Rush version (shown on the game's App Store page or in TestFlight), and describe what happened. A screenshot helps. Do not send passwords or payment details. We aim to answer within a few days.

Common questions

Do I have to watch ads?

No. The only ad is offered after you lose all your lives: tap Watch ad: +1 life to keep going, or choose Restart or Map, which never need an ad. There are no other ads.

No ad loads, or I'm offline. Can I still get the extra life?

No. The extra life comes only from watching an ad to the end, and ads need an internet connection. When no ad can be shown, connect to the internet and try again, or restart the level or go to the map. In Europe, the ads you can get also depend on your choice in Privacy choices.

What does ECC Mode give me?

ECC Mode is an optional weekly subscription: 5 lives instead of 3, and when you grab an empty device you lose 10% of the RAM you carry instead of 50%. It is not ad-free: the optional +1 life ad stays, and you never have to watch it.

How do I restore ECC Mode?

Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that bought ECC Mode and that you are online. Then tap Restore in the game.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › RAM Rush › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. ECC Mode stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the game does not cancel a subscription. More help: Apple's guide to cancelling a subscription.

I was charged but ECC Mode is not active, or I want a refund.

First try Restore (see above). Payments and refunds are handled by Apple, so we cannot refund you directly: request a refund from Apple. If ECC Mode still does not work, email us with the date of the purchase.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent message appears before any ad is requested. To change your answer later, tap Privacy choices in the game. Before the first ad, RAM Rush shows Apple's tracking prompt once; your answer changes nothing in the game, and you can change it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Your best results, cleared levels and the run you can resume are saved on your iPhone. There is no account to sign in to. If iCloud is on for the game, a copy of your best results, cleared levels, golden chips found and weekly challenge results is also kept in your own iCloud account, so a new iPhone signed in to the same iCloud account continues your datacenter (the run you were playing stays on the old iPhone, and settings are set again). Deleting the app deletes your progress from the iPhone; the iCloud copy stays until you remove it in Settings > [your name] > iCloud. We cannot see or recover either copy. A full iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) restored to a new iPhone may bring it back. Restoring ECC Mode does not restore game progress.

How do I report a bug?

Email us (see above) with your iPhone model, iOS version, RAM Rush version, the level or screen where it happened and what you expected to see. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
