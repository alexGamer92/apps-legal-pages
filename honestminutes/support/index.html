Honest Minutes Support

A plain page and an honest clock. Help for Honest Minutes on iPhone and iPad.

Contact us

For help, bug reports, privacy requests or questions, email Alexandru Struta at alexandrustruta@gmail.com. Please include your device model, the iOS or iPadOS version (Settings › General › About) and the Honest Minutes version (shown in the app under Settings › Version), and describe what happened. A screenshot helps. Please don't send your writing unless it's needed to show the problem. We aim to answer within a few days.

Common questions

How does the clock work?

Minutes count only while you are typing. The clock keeps running as long as keystrokes arrive and stops about 4 seconds after your last one. It also pauses when the app is in the background or the page isn't being edited. There is no start or stop button.

Why can't I paste?

Pasted text is ignored on purpose: the Paste menu, Cmd+V on a keyboard, Universal Clipboard and drag-and-drop. Any single insertion longer than 20 characters is also refused, and the app says “Pasted text is ignored”. Autocorrect, suggestions and Chinese or Japanese input work normally.

Does dictation count?

Dictation pauses the clock, and the app says “Dictation isn't counted”. Your dictated words stay on the page.

When does a new day start?

At 4 a.m. by default, so a session at 1 a.m. counts for the day before. Change it in Settings › Day starts at. Past days are read-only.

How is my streak counted?

Only days where you met your goal count. Today extends the streak once you meet today's goal; until then, yesterday's streak still stands.

Is it impossible to cheat?

No, and we won't claim it is. Honest Minutes ignores pasted text and pauses for dictation, which makes padding your minutes hard but not impossible. It's built for writers who want the number to mean something.

How do I restore Pro?

Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that bought Pro and that you are online. In the app, open Settings › Restore Purchases.

I was charged but Pro is not active, or I want a refund.

First try Restore Purchases. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple, so we cannot refund you directly: request a refund from Apple. If Pro still does not work, email us with the date of the purchase.

My widget doesn't update.

Widgets are part of Pro. They refresh when you write, when you meet your goal and when the day turns over. iOS decides the exact timing, so a widget can lag a little behind the app. Opening the app refreshes it.

How do I use Game Center?

It's optional. Open Settings › Game Center › Sign in to Game Center. Your best streak and total minutes then go to the Game Center leaderboards, and achievements unlock at 7, 30 and 100-day streaks and at 10 and 100 hours written. Your writing is never shared. To sign out, use the Settings app › Game Center.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes it all. Export your pages first (Pro) if you want to keep a copy.
